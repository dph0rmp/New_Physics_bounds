# Bounds on new physics from the spectroscopy of hydrogen and helium

This repository contains the details of some of the bounds reported in  

R M Potvliege, "Spectroscopy of light atoms and bounds on physics beyond the standard model", New Journal of Physics 27, 045002 (2025), https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/adc625 [open access] (preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.16315).

gegp_World_attr and gegp_World_rep: bounds on |g_eg_p| represented by the black solid line in, respectively, Fig. 2(a) and Fig. 2(b) of this article.

gegn_1s2s_attr and gegn_1s2s_rep: bounds on |g_eg_n| represented by the dashed blue line in, respectively, Fig. 3(a) and Fig. 3(b) of this article.
